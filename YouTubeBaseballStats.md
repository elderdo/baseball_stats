# 📺 Beginner-Friendly YouTube Guides

* [Simple Sabermetrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=no8dSQKw90s): This channel specializes in breaking down advanced concepts into plain English. Their Analytics for the Casual Fan video is an excellent starting point for understanding how evaluation has changed.
* Just Baseball: Offers short, punchy explanations of metrics like wRC+, explaining why a score of 100 is the most important number to remember.
* [Baseball Basics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=99uphIh2WfA): Features a deep dive into the "alphabet soup" of wOBA, wRC, and wRC+, showing how they build on each other to measure run creation. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

## 📖 Essential Reading for Casual Observers

* [MLB.com Sabermetrics Guide](https://www.mlb.com/news/sabermetrics-in-baseball-a-casual-fans-guide): An official, easy-to-read primer that explains the "why" behind modern stats and identifies which ones are most important for fans to know.
* [FanGraphs Library](https://library.fangraphs.com/offense/wrc/): While they host advanced data, their "Library" section provides simple scales for every stat. For example, their wRC+ Guide explicitly charts which numbers are "Poor," "Average," or "Elite".
* [Baseball Analytics for Beginners](https://baseballtips.com/baseball-sabermetrics-guide/baseball-analytics-for-beginners/): A practical breakdown that compares traditional stats like Batting Average to modern alternatives like OPS and FIP. [6, 7, 8, 9]

## 📊 Quick "Cheat Sheet" for Hitting Stats

If you see these metrics on a broadcast, here is the standard scale used by analysts to judge performance:

| Statistic [10, 11, 12] | What it Measures | Poor | Average | Elite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wRC+ | Overall Offensive Value | < 75 | 100 | 160+ |
| wOBA | Offensive Skill per PA | .290 | .320 | .400+ |
| OPS | On-Base + Slugging | .600 | .730 | .900+ |
